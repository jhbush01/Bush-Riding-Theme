About this route

4:30 we were up, forced down a banana. Two top layers and short cargo bib. Probably not going to be enough for the mid-winter Brisbane chill. It was 12 degrees but my fingers would have said differently. I live in this city and the route really starts in Wulkaraka. So we hopped on a train at Auchenflower station, 45 passed and we arrive at our routes real start point, Ipswich station.

You are now looking down the barrel of 85 kilometres of gravel. There are pretty towns in between, Fernvale, Lowood, Esk and Toogoolawah itself are quaint and I’m pretty sure they all have a bakery.

As the sun rose the dual layers were still needed under the chilly canopy of the Brisbane Valley Rail Trail. I stopped for a hotdog at the Lowood Bakery, Listened to the Ben & Email Show podcast about Cost-Co and how they are the most admirable and interesting companies in the world. And they really are! 30% higher than award wages, super matching and actually try to solve problems to keep their costs down instead of passing everything onto their loyal members. That burnt an hour in my mind.

Warning: from Coominya to Esk was all low gear for me, steady 2-4% constant incline.

Finished at the pub in Toogoolawah, not the one with the half-naked paintings on the wall, the one with the outdoor deck and insane steak sandwich. Also bought a Pilbara collection worker shirt from Dolly & Dust Country Clothing.

Vetted by Jarrod Hyslop.
