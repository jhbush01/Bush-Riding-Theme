About this route

If you want 60ish kms of lush, green countryside this route is for you. One steep climb 7kms from the Casino end of the rail trail and the rest is close to flat. We stopped at Two Mates Brewery at the Lismore end, the entrance is literally 2m from the trail and had their Red XPA. Delightful.

Vetted by Jarrod Hyslop.
